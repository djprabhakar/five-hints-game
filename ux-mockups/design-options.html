Five Hints — UX Design Options

A — Party Mode

Group-First

Bold, large-text design built for a shared screen. Teams take turns, scores are visible to all, and dramatic hint reveals create shared excitement. Inspired by Jackbox Games.

B — Game Show

Premium / Cinematic

Dark, dramatic, high-stakes feel. Hints reveal like sealed envelopes under a spotlight. Works for solo and groups. Think "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire" meets Wordle.

C — Clean & Casual

Accessible / Daily Play

Light, friendly, mobile-native feel. Clean card flips, subtle animations, easy to share. Best for regular solo players and casual groups. Inspired by NYT Games and Duolingo.
